0.3.0 — Scripts

Scripts
UAX #9, OpenType GSUB and GPOS, Arabic joining, Indic reordering.
shaping
Switch it off and the same strings fall back to one glyph per character, in codepoint order.
Arabic
اللغة العربية
Every letter takes an initial, medial, final or isolated form from what it joins to. GSUB does it; the C here passes a string.
The lam-alef ligature
لا
Two codepoints, one glyph, required of every Arabic font.
Hebrew
שלום
A number inside right-to-left text
السعر 1250 دولار
The digits run left to right inside a line that runs right to left. This is the case UAX #9 exists for.
Devanagari
हिन्दी
The vowel sign is typed after its consonant and drawn before it. Reordering happens before the font is asked for anything.
